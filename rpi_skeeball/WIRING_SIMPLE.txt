SKEEBALL WIRING - SIMPLE DIAGRAM
=================================

POWER SUPPLIES:
  12V PSU ──┬─► 12V+ Rail
            └─► GND Rail
  
  5V PSU ──┬─► Raspberry Pi (USB-C)
           ├─► Optocoupler Modules (VCC)
           └─► Relay Module (VCC)


SWITCHES → OPTOCOUPLERS → RASPBERRY PI:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  [Switch]     [Optocoupler]      [Pi GPIO]
  ────────     ─────────────      ─────────
  
  Coin    ──►  Opto IN1  ──OUT──► GPIO 17
  Ball    ──►  Opto IN2  ──OUT──► GPIO 18
  10pt    ──►  Opto IN3  ──OUT──► GPIO 22
  20pt    ──►  Opto IN4  ──OUT──► GPIO 23
  30pt    ──►  Opto IN5  ──OUT──► GPIO 24
  40pt    ──►  Opto IN6  ──OUT──► GPIO 25
  50pt    ──►  Opto IN7  ──OUT──► GPIO 27
  100L    ──►  Opto IN8  ──OUT──► GPIO 19
  100R    ──►  Opto IN9  ──OUT──► GPIO 13

All switch COM terminals → 12V+ (common rail)


RASPBERRY PI → RELAY → OUTPUTS:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  [Pi GPIO]     [Relay Module]      [Load]
  ─────────     ──────────────      ──────
  
  GPIO 10  ──►  Relay 1 IN  ──COM──► 12V+
                            ──NO───► Solenoid (+)
                                     Solenoid (-) → GND
                                     [1N4007 diode across solenoid]
  
  GPIO 26  ──►  Relay 2 IN  ──COM──► 12V+
                            ──NO───► LED Power (optional)


DISPLAYS (Direct connection):
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  Display 1:                Display 2:
    VCC → Pi 5V               VCC → Pi 5V
    GND → Pi GND              GND → Pi GND
    CLK → GPIO 5              CLK → GPIO 11
    DIO → GPIO 6              DIO → GPIO 9

QUICK INSTALL CHECKLIST:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

□ Mount all modules on board/DIN rail
□ Wire power distribution (12V + 5V)
□ Connect optocoupler modules to Pi 5V
□ Wire all switches to optocoupler inputs
□ Wire optocoupler outputs to Pi GPIOs
□ Connect relay module to Pi 5V
□ Wire Pi GPIOs to relay inputs
□ Wire relay outputs to solenoid
□ Add flyback diode across solenoid
□ Connect displays to Pi

□ Test continuity before power on
□ Power test (no load)
□ Test each switch individually
□ Test relays
□ Full system test


NOTES:
━━━━━

• All switch COM terminals connect to 12V+ rail (common)
• Optocouplers provide electrical isolation
• Relay module has built-in transistor drivers
• Always add flyback diode across solenoid
• Use 22 AWG or larger wire
• Keep 12V and 5V systems separated
• Test in stages, don't power everything at once
