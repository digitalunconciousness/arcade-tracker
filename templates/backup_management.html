Backups run outside the app

This tracker runs on PostgreSQL. The buttons that used to be here copied a SQLite file that production doesn't use, so they're off until in-app PostgreSQL backups exist.

  • deploy/deploy.sh takes a full dump before every deploy and keeps the newest 14 in {{ deploy_dir }} on the tracker's server.
  • To restore one, follow "Rollback" in deploy/RUNBOOK.md. It's a server-side job on purpose: a restore replaces everything.
  • For a backup right now, on the server, with .env loaded: pg_dump --clean --if-exists --no-owner --no-privileges "$DATABASE_URL" | gzip -9 > {{ deploy_dir }}/manual-$(date -u +%Y%m%dT%H%M%SZ).sql.gz
